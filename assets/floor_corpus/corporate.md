Team,

As part of our ongoing commitment to operational excellence, we are pleased to announce the rollout of a new knowledge management platform across the organization. This initiative reflects our strategic priority of empowering teams to capture, surface, and leverage institutional knowledge more effectively.

Beginning next quarter, all departments will transition to the new platform. The migration will be conducted in phases to minimize disruption to business-critical workflows. Department leads will receive detailed enablement materials, and dedicated office hours will be available throughout the transition period to ensure alignment and address any questions that may arise.

We recognize that change of this nature requires adjustment, and we appreciate your flexibility and partnership as we work through the implementation. Early pilot results have been encouraging, with participating teams reporting meaningful improvements in information discoverability and cross-functional collaboration.

Please direct any questions to your department lead or to the project team via the dedicated support channel. Additional details, including the full migration timeline and training schedule, will be shared in the coming weeks.

Thank you for your continued dedication as we invest in the tools and processes that position us for long-term success.
